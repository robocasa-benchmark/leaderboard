- Notes: MIRA is a hierarchical System2 + System1 policy. System2, a Qwen3-VL-4B planner fine-tuned from Qwen3-VL-4B (checkpoint-17124, [https://huggingface.co/yinpei-tri-pi/robocasa-system2-qwen3vl-4b](https://huggingface.co/yinpei-tri-pi/robocasa-system2-qwen3vl-4b), served with vLLM 0.19.1, greedy decoding), writes a milestone plan and, after every System1 segment, watches a clip of it and issues the next subgoal. System1, a subgoal-conditioned policy fine-tuned from pi0.5 (step 269999, [https://huggingface.co/yinpei-tri-pi/robocasa-system1-pi05-progact](https://huggingface.co/yinpei-tri-pi/robocasa-system1-pi05-progact)), outputs action chunks plus its own subgoal-progress estimate (progress-as-action). Hardcoded language-steering rules revise System2's output before it reaches System1. training_config refers to System1. Policy observations only; no privileged simulator state or success signal is used during an episode, and there is no cross-episode memory. Evaluation follows the official robocasa-benchmark/openpi protocol: RoboCasa 1.0.1, split=pretrain, seed 7, 50 sequential trials per task, per-task registry horizon. Results contain 1,358 successes over 2,500 episodes with 0 infrastructure errors: Atomic-Seen 721/900, Composite-Seen 412/800, Composite-Unseen 225/800 (task-weighted overall 54.3). Per-task results and the full setup are in doc/results.md of the code repo.

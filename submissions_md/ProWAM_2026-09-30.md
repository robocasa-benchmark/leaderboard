- Notes: ProWAM: World Action Modeling with Progressive Visual Planning. A 5B world action model that predicts an ordered chain of sparse sub-goals indexed by a normalized task-progress coordinate, then conditions action prediction on that chain. Sub-goals share the video backbone and are distinguished by a progress-conditioned adaLN offset; the video branch never attends to action tokens, so one cached video-expert pass serves every action denoising step. Evaluated with K=6 sub-goals at progress 0.3/0.4/0.5/0.65/0.8/0.9, action horizon 32, replan every 32 steps, 10 denoising steps, 50 official target tasks.
